Summer Research — Trans-Resolution 3D Printing

Institution: Bioprinting and Regeneration Engineering Laboratory, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University
Supervisor: Professor Zhuo Xiong
Duration: June – August 2024

Laboratory Homepage (BMC, Tsinghua University)
Patent: CN119748849A — Trans-Resolution 3D Printing System

Overview

During my 2024 summer research internship at the Bioprinting and Regeneration Engineering Laboratory (BMC Lab), Tsinghua University, I contributed to a research project focusing on the development of a trans-resolution 3D printing mechanism—an innovative system enabling dynamic adjustment of print resolution within a single manufacturing process.

The research aimed to overcome the conventional trade-off between printing precision and speed, exploring new methods of resolution switching and real-time control within a single extrusion framework.

My Role and Contributions

1. Mechanical Design Support

I participated in the mechanical design and optimization of the trans-resolution printing mechanism.

  • Collaborated on the conceptual and detailed design of the variable-resolution extrusion module, ensuring smooth resolution transitions under continuous material flow.
  • Utilized SolidWorks for structural modeling, assembly design, and component stress analysis.
  • Focused on achieving a balance between mechanical precision, lightweight structure, and thermal management for sustained high-quality output.

2. Printing Result Preview Application

In addition to mechanical contributions, I developed a printing result preview application to assist researchers in visualizing the print trajectory and expected surface morphology.

  • The tool provided real-time 3D previews of printing paths and estimated material distribution based on input process parameters.
  • Implemented using C# and OpenGL-based rendering, improving workflow efficiency and design validation within the research team.

Patent Reference

Patent Title: Trans-Resolution 3D Printing System
Patent Number: CN119748849A
Applicant: Tsinghua University
Contributors: Zhuo Xiong, Shaotong Hong, et al.
Publication Year: 2025
Patent Summary: The invention presents a trans-resolution additive manufacturing system capable of dynamic spatial resolution adjustment during the printing process, enabling both micro- and macro-scale precision within a unified mechanical architecture.

Confidentiality Statement

Due to the confidential and pre-publication nature of the ongoing research, only limited information about the project mechanism and technical results can be shared publicly.
Further details regarding system architecture, control algorithms, and experimental outcomes are subject to laboratory confidentiality agreements and cannot be disclosed at this stage.

Reflection

This research experience allowed me to apply my skills in mechanical system design, software visualization, and collaborative research within a leading academic environment.
It deepened my understanding of additive manufacturing, mechatronics integration, and the interplay between precision engineering and biological applications.
The work not only contributed to a patent publication but also reinforced my long-term interest in robotics and intelligent manufacturing systems.

“Innovation in manufacturing begins when precision meets adaptability.”
