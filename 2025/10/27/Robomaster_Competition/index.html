RoboMaster Competition Experience

RoboMaster Official Website

Overview

RoboMaster is a nationwide robotics competition organized by DJI, bringing together university teams across China to design, build, and operate a fleet of robots for technical challenges and strategic battles. The competition emphasizes interdisciplinary collaboration, combining mechanical engineering, electronics, embedded systems, and computer vision. It has become one of the most influential robotics events for university students, encouraging innovation, teamwork, and hands-on engineering excellence.

My Journey with XJTLU GMaster Team

I joined the GMaster Team of Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) in 2021, starting as a mechanical group member with no prior experience in robotics design.
From the very beginning, I devoted myself to learning SolidWorks and quickly integrated into the team’s R&D workflow. I participated in the optimization and upgrade of the existing competition robots, focusing on mechanical structure refinement to improve the gimbal’s response speed and structural strength.

In 2022, despite the dual challenges of academic pressure and COVID-19 restrictions, I continued contributing to robot design and successfully took part in the RoboMaster National Competition, where our team achieved the First Prize (National Level).
This milestone not only strengthened my technical foundation but also taught me perseverance and effective collaboration under pressure.

Following this achievement, I was elected as the Mechanical Group Leader in 2023. In this role, I coordinated multiple R&D sub-projects simultaneously, managing limited resources while adapting to significant rule changes in the 2023 season.
Under my leadership, the team successfully maintained steady progress and ensured participation in the season’s competition despite tight deadlines and technical uncertainties.

In late 2023, due to my participation in the University of Liverpool 2+2 Exchange Program, I stepped down from my position and concluded my active role in the GMaster Team.
However, the experience I gained in RoboMaster continues to shape my approach to robot design, project coordination, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Summary

My three years in RoboMaster provided a transformative journey from a beginner to a team leader.
Through continuous learning, technical innovation, and teamwork, I developed strong skills in mechanical design, system integration, and leadership—all of which became the foundation for my ongoing pursuit of robotics research and development.

“Building robots is not only about engineering — it’s about learning to work as a team, innovate under constraints, and turn imagination into motion.”
